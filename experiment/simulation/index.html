Step 1: Understanding Peterson's Solution

Learn the foundational concepts: mutual exclusion, progress, and bounded waiting.

Objective:

Identify the two processes, both flag variables, and the turn variable in the simulation visualizer.

Instructions:

Observe the simulation layout. Two processes (P0 and P1) compete for a critical section. The algorithm uses flag[0], flag[1] (intent signals) and turn (priority variable). Read the Legend in the Controls panel to understand each state color: gray=Inactive, blue=Active, yellow=Waiting, green=Critical Section.

Hint:

The Controls panel on the left shows the Legend. The visualizer (green-bordered card) shows P0, P1, flag[0], flag[1], and turn indicators.
